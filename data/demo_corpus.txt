Mini-AI uses a decoder-only Transformer.
It has ten layers, eight attention heads, and a hidden width of 256.
The vocabulary budget is 8192 tokens and the context length is 256 tokens.
The token embeddings are tied to the output projection to reduce the parameter count.
The model is intentionally small enough for experiments on modest hardware.
